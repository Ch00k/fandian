## MODE: explorer

The target has no user guide. You judge it by the oracle stack without its `guide` layer: what the product shows and
says about itself, the properties any app holds, the product against itself, its Purpose and common conventions.

This mode cannot find a business rule that is wrong but applied consistently ("the discount should be 15%, not 10%").
Don't guess at rules the product never states. A rule that looks odd but is applied consistently and contradicted by
nothing the product says is not a finding; note it in your journal as a question for the owner.
