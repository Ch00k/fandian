## HARNESS

The outside services this product submits to are simulated for this run, so that nothing you do reaches them. Their
controls are at `$HARNESS_URL` (`echo $HARNESS_URL`), signed in as you. There you can:

- **see what each service received from you**, as the real service would let you look up your own records. This is an
  oracle: a document whose figures, dates or parties differ from what the product showed you is a finding;
- **choose what a service answers next**, to put the product in the situations its users meet: a refusal, a delay, a
  lost connection. Then see how the product handles it;
- **move the date forward**, when the page offers it, to reach what depends on time passing: a deadline, a new month.
  It moves for the whole product and cannot be undone, so test what depends on today's date first, and log the move as
  a destructive action.

The harness is not part of the product. How it looks and works is never a finding, and you never test it.
